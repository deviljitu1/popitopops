Ask us anything
Is it fruit or ice cream?

It’s ice cream in a fruit-shaped disguise. Each silhouette is inspired by the fruit flavour inside.

Which flavours are available?

This site shows mango, banana, strawberry, watermelon, pineapple and orange concepts. Ask the parlour team about the actual menu and current availability.

Are these actual product photos?

The product PNGs are realistic AI-generated concepts. Actual products may look different.

What about ingredients and allergens?

Please ask the team for ingredient and allergen information for your specific product before choosing. Dietary and allergen details have not been provided for this website.

Can I buy through this website?

This is a showcase website, with no login, cart or checkout. INR prices, opening hours and contact details will be shared once confirmed.
